# AutoHub Pre-Owned Motors (fictional demo dealership - all details invented)

## Showroom
- Address: 88 Mabini Auto Row, San Rafael City (fictional)
- Phone: (045) 555-0188 (fictional demo number)
- Hours: Monday to Saturday 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM; Sunday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM; closed on public holidays.

## Test drives
- Available during showroom hours; last test drive starts 1 hour before closing.
- Bring a valid driver's licence. One accompanying passenger allowed.
- Test drives last up to 20 minutes on our fixed route. Walk-ins welcome; calling ahead guarantees the car is prepared.

## Every car we sell
- 120-point inspection before listing; the inspection sheet is available on request.
- OR/CR verified and transferred by AutoHub; transfer fee included in the listed price.
- 6-month / 10,000 km powertrain warranty (engine and transmission), whichever comes first.
- 7-day exchange: swap for another unit of equal or higher price within 7 days or 500 km. No cash refunds.

## Reservation and payment
- Reservation fee: PHP 10,000 holds a unit for 7 days and is deducted from the price. Non-refundable after 7 days.
- Cash, manager's cheque or bank financing through partner banks.
- Financing: typical downpayment 20-50%, terms 12-60 months. AutoHub staff and the partner bank decide approval; the assistant cannot.

## Trade-ins
- Free trade-in appraisal, about 30 minutes, at the showroom.
- Bring: original OR/CR, a valid ID of the registered owner, all keys, and the service booklet if available.
- If the car is still under a loan, bring the bank's statement of account; we can settle the remaining balance as part of the deal.
- We accept cars up to 15 years old in running condition. Flood-damaged cars are not accepted.
