# Presentation script — AutoHub Assistant (target 6–7 minutes)

Record your screen with your voice (OBS, Loom, or Windows Game Bar `Win+Alt+R`). Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**,
copy the video ID (the part after `watch?v=`), put it in `site/pages/index.html` in place of `VIDEO_ID`, run
`python3 site/build.py`, commit and push. Before recording: close Gmail tabs you don't want shown, and **never open
Parameter Store values, AI Studio keys or `.env`** on screen.
