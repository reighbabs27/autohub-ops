SYSTEM CARD: AutoHub Assistant

1. What it does

AutoHub Assistant is a chat help desk for a fictional used-car dealership. It answers questions about the cars in stock, prices, test drives, trade-ins, reservations and the steps of financing, using only the dealership's own information.

2. Who uses it, and who must not

  • Intended users: customers browsing the dealership's stock and policies, and showroom staff checking a detail quickly.
  • Explicitly NOT for: deciding or predicting whether a person gets a loan or credit; judging anyone's income, credit score or eligibility; collecting personal details; legal or mechanical advice about a specific vehicle's condition; any decision about an identifiable individual.

3. Base system

  • Name and version: LibreChat, main branch, build f4154b6 (31 Oct 2026), Docker image librechat-dev:latest
  • Source URL: github.com/LibreChat-AI/LibreChat · Author: Danny Avila and contributors · Licence: MIT
  • What it did originally: a general-purpose ChatGPT-style interface over many AI providers, with users, conversations, search, agents, file upload and RAG.

5. Data

  • Where it came from: written by us for this project (content/01-dealership.md, content/02-inventory.md). Licence: ours, invented.
  • Size and format: 2 Markdown files, about 3.5 KB: 12 vehicles × 10 fields, plus showroom, test-drive, warranty, reservation, financing and trade-in policies.
  • Anything personal in it: no, all invented. The phone number, address and dealership are fictional.
  • Sent to a third-party AI service: every question and the dealership content are sent to Google Gemini. All of it is invented. Free-tier prompts may be used by Google to improve its products, which is why no real data may be entered.

6. How good it is

  • The one number: __EVAL_SCORE__ correct (__EVAL_PCT__) on our labelled test set.
  • How we measured it: app/eval.py sends 30 labelled questions to the model with the live system prompt. Each answer is checked automatically for required facts and forbidden phrases. The set covers 12 inventory lookups, 12 policy questions, 3 guardrail cases and 3 out-of-scope questions.

7. How it is served

  • Where it runs: one EC2 t3.small (Amazon Linux 2023, Docker) in ap-southeast-2 (Sydney), behind CloudFront for HTTPS.
  • What calls it: the browser calls https://dd56jth94yne6.cloudfront.net/api/…. LibreChat then calls Google's generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/models/gemini-3.1-flash-lite:streamGenerateContent.
  • The model: Google gemini-3.1-flash-lite, free tier, key held in Parameter Store. (We started on gemini-2.5-flash, which was retired, then gemini-3.5-flash, whose free daily quota was too small: see INC-02 and INC-05.)
  • If the model is unavailable: the chat shows the provider's error (for example 429 quota exhausted, 503 overloaded or 404 retired model) and no answer. The site stays up and conversations are kept. The next step on our list is a custom metric that alarms on failed answers.

8. Limitations and risks

  • What it cannot do: it does not know about cars or policies that are not in the content files. It cannot book, reserve or take payment. It cannot see real-time stock changes until the content in S3 is updated and the app redeployed.
  • What could go wrong with the data: the stock list in S3 can go stale, and the assistant would then quote a sold car. The model can also misread a table.
  • How it could be misused: someone could try to make it promise a price, a discount or a loan approval. The system prompt forbids this, and the evaluation tests it.
  • What a user must always check themselves: price, availability and every financing term, with showroom staff, before paying anything.

9. Operational summary

  • Where the code lives: the autohub-ops Git repository (scripts, server config, content, this site).
  • How often it is backed up: MongoDB every 6 hours to S3, kept 8 days. Content in S3 is versioned.
  • How we know it is healthy: a health check every 5 minutes, 7 CloudWatch alarms emailing the operator, and the autohub-operations dashboard.
